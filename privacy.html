Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

When the owner authorises it, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and contacts) through Google's APIs, only to carry out the owner's requests.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. The access token is stored locally on that computer.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Parts of the data may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only to answer the owner's own requests.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
